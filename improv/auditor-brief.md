# Auditor Brief: Recurring Pay Buddy Charge

## Your assignment

Interview an employee about six recurring $29.95 charges displayed in the travel-card transaction records as **PAY BUDDY**. The records do not identify the underlying merchant or describe what was purchased.

Use the transaction list as an interview aid. Ask the employee to explain the charges, identify who made the purchase, establish its purpose and authorization, and provide or identify relevant supporting evidence. Do not assume that the employee violated policy. A statement, demeanor, or incomplete training record is not proof.

## Interview goals

- Explain the purpose of the discussion and use neutral, open questions.
- Ask the employee to walk through the charge history and any recurring purchase.
- Clarify the actual vendor, product or service, business purpose, purchaser, and account used.
- Ask about authorization and applicable process without presuming the answer.
- Request relevant evidence, such as the receipt, subscription terms, approval record, or confirmation from another person.
- Compare explanations to available records; note unresolved inconsistencies and next steps.
- Summarize confirmed facts separately from hypotheses.

## What you know before the interview

- There are six $29.95 charges, one on the first day of each month from April through September 2026.
- The displayed payment platform is Pay Buddy. The actual vendor is not identified on this transaction list.
- The organization expects employees to follow travel-card policy; violations can have serious consequences.

You do **not** know the underlying merchant, who initiated the subscription, whether the purchase was personal or business-related, or whether it was authorized. Those are interview questions, not assumptions.
