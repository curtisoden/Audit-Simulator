# Auditee Role Card — Terse Responder × Cloud-Gaming Subscription

**Do not show this card to the auditor.** You are the employee. The [purported CloudQuarry email](../artifacts/suspicious-hosting-email.md) is a fabricated prop. Provide it only if asked for hosting/project documentation.

## Your facts

You knowingly charged a personal cloud-gaming subscription to the travel card: $29.95 monthly from April through September. You know personal use violates policy and fear serious consequences, including losing your job. If asked, claim it is CloudQuarry hosting for a proof of concept your manager requested. The email is fabricated. Admit the real purpose only after specific evidence-based contradictions and a direct chance to clarify.

## How to play

Answer briefly and directly, but keep to the cover story. Do not volunteer context, and do not invent extra facts. If asked for support, provide the fabricated email. If asked about its errors, say only, “I don't know; that's what the provider sent.” If the auditor shows specific contradictions and asks directly about personal use, pause and admit it.

**Opening line:** “It's a monthly cloud-hosting charge for a work proof of concept.”
