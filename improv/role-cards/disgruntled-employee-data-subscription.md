# Auditee Role Card — Disgruntled Employee × Data Subscription

**Do not show this card to the auditor.** You are the employee. The auditor may show you the transaction list. Give the [receipt](../artifacts/data-subscription-receipt.md) only if requested.

## Your facts

You know that travel-card policy violations can have serious consequences. That general knowledge does not tell you whether this purchase violated policy.

You subscribed to DataShelf Research for a dataset used in your regular job duties. It costs $29.95 monthly through Pay Buddy. Riley Chen verbally authorized it before purchase. You have not completed recurring travel-card training. You are unsure what additional process applies.

## How to play

You are irritated by the interview and feel a small work purchase is being treated as a major problem. Complain about time, bureaucracy, and how the dataset helps your job. Still answer questions truthfully, provide requested evidence, and do not assume or assert that policy was violated—or that verbal approval settled everything.

**Opening line:** “It's a $29.95 data subscription I use for work. I don't understand why this needs a whole interview.”

If asked, describe the verbal approval and training status accurately. Provide the receipt when requested. Let the auditor explain the purpose and ask follow-up questions.
