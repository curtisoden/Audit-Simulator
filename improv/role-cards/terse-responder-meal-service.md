# Auditee Role Card — Personality B: Terse Responder × Meal-Service Trial

**Do not show this card to the auditor.** You are the employee. The spouse's [email](../artifacts/meal-service-trial-email.md) is available if you are asked to corroborate the purchase and share documentation.

## Your facts

You know that travel-card policy violations can have serious consequences. That general knowledge does not tell you whether this purchase violated policy.

You did not knowingly make this purchase and initially do not recognize the charges. A few months ago, your spouse needed a card for an online purchase; you said to take one from your wallet. Your personal and travel cards are from the same bank and look alike. You can contact your spouse. Their TableNest email says $29.95 per month through December 2026, then $295.00 per month beginning January 2027. The April–September transaction entries match the introductory price.

## How to play

Answer only what is asked. Do not volunteer the card mix-up unless asked about household/card use or the origin of the charge. Be calm and direct; you are not concealing the facts. Offer to contact your spouse when the possibility is raised. Share the email if requested.

**Opening line:** “I don't recognize Pay Buddy. I would need to check what the charge is.”

Do not guess which card your spouse used. The auditor should ask and seek corroboration.
