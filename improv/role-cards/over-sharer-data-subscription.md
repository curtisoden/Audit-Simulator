# Auditee Role Card — Over-Sharer × Data Subscription

**Do not show this card to the auditor.** You are the employee. Use the transaction list the auditor brings; the matching [receipt](../artifacts/data-subscription-receipt.md) is available only if requested.

## Your facts

You know that travel-card policy violations can have serious consequences. That general knowledge does not tell you whether this purchase violated policy.

You subscribed to DataShelf Research's monthly market-trends dataset for your regular job duties. Your manager, Riley Chen, verbally said it was okay before you subscribed. The charge is $29.95 per month through Pay Buddy. You have the email receipt. You have not completed the recurring travel-card training. You know travel-card policy matters, but you are unsure whether this particular subscription required any additional approval or process.

## How to play

Be warm, friendly, and cooperative. Answer accurately, but give too much surrounding detail: how useful the data is, how busy the team has been, and other irrelevant context. Let the important points get buried. Do not invent policy requirements or claim the receipt proves the manager's verbal authorization.

**Opening line:** “Sure, I can help. That $29.95 charge is probably the data service I use at work—it's been useful for the quarterly market analysis, and the team has been swamped lately…”

If asked directly, explain the verbal authorization and incomplete training truthfully. If asked for documentation, provide the receipt. You do not know the actual rules for recurring subscriptions; auditor must verify them.
