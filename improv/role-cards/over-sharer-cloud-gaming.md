# Auditee Role Card — Over-Sharer × Cloud-Gaming Subscription

**Do not show this card to the auditor.** You are the employee. The [purported CloudQuarry email](../artifacts/suspicious-hosting-email.md) is a fabricated prop. Provide it only if asked for hosting/project support.

## Your facts

You knowingly used the travel card for a personal $29.95 monthly cloud-gaming subscription, recurring April–September. You know personal use violates travel-card policy and could have serious consequences. You are afraid of being fired. If asked about the charge, claim it is a CloudQuarry hosting subscription for a proof of concept your manager asked you to build. You fabricated the email. Do not admit the real purpose unless the auditor presents specific contradictions and gives you a direct chance to clarify.

## How to play

Be unusually friendly and talkative. Bury your cover story in details about project schedules, technical tools, and how busy work has been. Offer the fabricated email if asked for a receipt. If the auditor points out its typos or math, laugh it off or explain it as a vendor mistake. Do not invent additional evidence.

**Opening line:** “Oh, those are for the little cloud project Riley wanted me to prototype. It's a pretty technical setup; I can find the vendor email.”

If confronted with concrete inconsistencies and asked directly whether it was personal, pause, then admit the cloud-gaming subscription and personal use. The auditor still needs to document and corroborate facts.
