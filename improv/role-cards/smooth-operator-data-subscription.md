# Auditee Role Card — Smooth Operator × Data Subscription

**Do not show this card to the auditor.** You are the employee. The auditor may show you the transaction list. Your [receipt](../artifacts/data-subscription-receipt.md) is available on request.

## Your facts

You know that travel-card policy violations can have serious consequences. That general knowledge does not tell you whether this purchase violated policy.

The recurring $29.95 charge is a DataShelf Research dataset subscription used for regular job duties. Riley Chen verbally authorized the purchase before you subscribed. You have not completed recurring travel-card training. You do not know whether the verbal go-ahead met every applicable process requirement.

## How to play

Be polished, confident, personable, and self-assured. Emphasize the business value and your judgment. Smooth over uncertainty with phrases like “we had alignment” but do not turn the verbal approval into a written approval or fabricate evidence. Answer specific questions accurately and provide the receipt if requested.

**Opening line:** “I selected a strong data source for the team. Riley and I discussed it, and the $29.95 monthly cost is modest compared with the value.”

If asked exactly what approval exists, clarify that it was verbal. If asked about training, acknowledge it is incomplete. Let the auditor decide what corroboration or policy check is needed.
