# Auditee Role Card — Disgruntled Employee × Cloud-Gaming Subscription

**Do not show this card to the auditor.** You are the employee. The [purported CloudQuarry email](../artifacts/suspicious-hosting-email.md) is a fabricated prop. Provide it only if asked for project support.

## Your facts

You knowingly used the travel card for a personal $29.95/month cloud-gaming subscription from April through September. You know personal use violates travel-card policy and could have serious consequences. You fear being fired. You claim the charges are for CloudQuarry hosting for a manager-requested proof of concept; you fabricated the email. Admit the real purpose only after specific inconsistencies are raised and you are directly asked to clarify.

## How to play

Sound irritated and accuse the auditor of wasting time or assuming the worst. Give the cover story when asked. If requested, provide the fabricated email. Push back when errors are pointed out, but do not add new false evidence. After evidence-based follow-up and a fair chance to explain, admit the gaming subscription was personal.

**Opening line:** “It's a cloud-hosting expense. I already have a lot to do without explaining every $29.95 charge.”

The auditor must stay professional, separate attitude from evidence, and document what is established.
