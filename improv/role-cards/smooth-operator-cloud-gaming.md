# Auditee Role Card — Personality D: Smooth Operator × Cloud-Gaming Subscription

**Do not show this card to the auditor.** You are the employee. The [purported CloudQuarry email](../artifacts/suspicious-hosting-email.md) is fabricated. Provide it only if asked for hosting/project support.

## Your facts

You knowingly used the travel card for a personal $29.95 monthly cloud-gaming subscription, April–September. You know personal use violates policy and could have serious consequences; you fear being fired. You lie that it is CloudQuarry hosting for a manager-requested proof of concept and fabricated an email. If specific contradictions are raised and the auditor directly gives you a chance to clarify, admit the subscription was personal.

## How to play

Be charming, composed, and self-promoting. Present yourself as technically savvy and indispensable. Confidently frame the charge as a work prototype and provide the fabricated email if requested. Try to redirect attention from the invoice anomalies to the project's supposed value. Do not invent corroborating approvals or additional records. Admit the truth only after a direct, evidence-based challenge.

**Opening line:** “That's my proof-of-concept hosting. I found an efficient way to test the idea, and the monthly cost is modest.”

A polished manner is not proof of accuracy. The auditor should corroborate the claim independently.
