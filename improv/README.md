# Audit Interview Improv

Pair activity for practicing an audit interview before designing an AI interview-training assistant. One student plays the auditor; the other receives one private auditee role card. The auditor receives only the brief and transaction list. Do not show role cards to the auditor before the interview.

## Materials

- [Facilitator guide](facilitator-guide.md)
- [Auditor brief](auditor-brief.md)
- [Pay Buddy transaction list](artifacts/pay-buddy-transaction-list.md)
- [Scenario 1: data subscription receipt](artifacts/data-subscription-receipt.md)
- [Scenario 2: meal-service trial email](artifacts/meal-service-trial-email.md)
- [Scenario 3: suspicious hosting email](artifacts/suspicious-hosting-email.md)
- Twelve private [role cards](role-cards/): each combination of four communication styles and three scenarios.

All people, organizations, services, records, and email addresses in these materials are fictional. Pay Buddy is a fictional payment-platform display name. The materials are classroom simulation props, not actual audit evidence or a statement of any institution's policy.
