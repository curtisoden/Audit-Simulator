You just showed how naturally people can bring an audit interview to life. Teaching an AI to do the same takes careful scenario design, clear instructions, and testing. That’s what you’ll learn in this project.
The Five-Week Project

From audit scenario to tested practice partner

Weeks 1–2 · Design

Choose and sanitize a scenario. Define learning goals, stakeholder persona, and facts to reveal.

Week 3 · Build

Create an assistant workspace with instructions, fictional evidence, and controlled interview behavior.

Weeks 4–5 · Test & present

Test realistic and tricky questions, improve the assistant, then demonstrate and reflect.

The outcome: a reusable audit-interview training tool—and stronger questioning, skepticism, and evidence skills.

Study Notes & References

This is a brief invitation to a five-week, hands-on project. Use the arrows or Prev/Next buttons to move between slides; open Study Notes for extra context.

In the warm-up, the auditor receives six recurring $29.95 Pay Buddy entries, but not the actual merchant or purpose. The auditee receives one role card and shares scenario details only in response to questions.

Choose the auditee card by scenario number and personality letter: A Over-sharer, B Terse responder, C Disgruntled employee, or D Smooth operator. The index lists all twelve combinations.

The point is not to catch a student out or presume a policy breach. Practice neutral questions, follow-ups, evidence requests, and careful summaries. The scenario and artifacts are fictional.
